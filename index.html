DeepOS: a free focus timer with a brain dump

Dump the noise, keep working. Pick one task, start a 15, 25 or 45 minute focus session, and when a distracting thought shows up, write it down in one line and get back to work.

How it works

  • Pick one thing. Type your task on the timer and press Start.
  • Dump what buzzes. Any thought, to-do or worry goes into your brain dump in one line.
  • Sort it later. After the session, start each thought or let it go.

Pop the timer out so it floats above your other apps, see your focused time in Analytics, and install it as an app. No account, no sign-up: your tasks and thoughts stay in your browser. Free and open source.
